Before you book

Straight Answers

Is 20 minutes really enough?

It's longer than it sounds. Swinging a sledgehammer in coveralls is genuinely hard work. Most people are winded by minute twelve and grinning by minute fifteen. If you want a full hour, book the private party room.

Is it safe?

Safety is the whole reason the room exists. Before you swing anything you'll gear up in full protective equipment: coveralls, heavy-duty gloves, protective eyewear and a face shield helmet. The room is enclosed, supervised and built for exactly this.

What do I need to wear or bring?

Closed-toe shoes are required: no sandals, crocs, heels or flimsy flats. Bring a valid ID and wear clothes you don't mind getting messy. We provide everything else.

How old do you have to be?

Ages 14 and up. Anyone under 18 needs a parent or guardian present, who also signs the liability waiver.

Do I need a reservation?

Walk-ins are welcome whenever we have an open slot, but availability isn't guaranteed, especially evenings and weekends. Booking ahead takes a minute and locks in your time.

Can I bring my own stuff to smash?

Often yes, with approval first. Email us and we'll let you know. For safety we can't allow pressurised cans, light bulbs, hazardous materials, heating or cooling devices, or old CRT televisions.

Do you host parties and events?

Constantly. Birthdays, bachelorette and bachelor parties, team building, unit outings and full facility buyouts. Book the private party room above, or email wreckitroomvb@gmail.com and we'll build a package around your group.

Are there any discounts?

Active duty and veterans get 10% off with valid ID. Gift vouchers are available online any time.
