Before you book

Straight Answers

Is 20 minutes really enough?

It's longer than it sounds. Swinging a bat in coveralls is genuinely hard work. Most people are winded by minute twelve and grinning by minute fifteen. If you want a full hour, book a group session.

Is it safe?

Safety is the whole reason the room exists. Before you swing anything you'll gear up in full protective equipment: coveralls, heavy-duty gloves, protective eyewear and a face shield helmet. The room is enclosed, supervised and built for exactly this.

What do I need to wear or bring?

Closed-toe shoes are required: no sandals, crocs, heels or flimsy flats. Wear clothes you don't mind getting messy. We provide everything else.

How old do you have to be?

The rage room is 14 and up. The paint room is all ages — that's the one the whole family can do together. Anyone under 18 needs a parent or guardian present, who also signs the liability waiver.

Do I need a reservation?

Monday to Thursday, yes — we run by reservation only. Friday to Sunday we take walk-ins whenever we have an open slot, but availability isn't guaranteed and weekends fill up fast. Booking ahead takes a minute and locks in your time.

Can I bring my own stuff to smash?

Often yes, with approval first — there's a small fee for bringing your own items. Email us and we'll let you know. For safety we can't allow pressurised cans, light bulbs, hazardous materials, heating or cooling devices, or old CRT televisions.

Do you host parties and events?

Constantly. Birthdays, bachelorette and bachelor parties, team building, unit outings and full facility buyouts. Every session is already in its own private room, so there's no separate party room to book — the group sessions above simply give you a bigger room, a full hour and a lot more to break. Book one above, or email wreckitroomvb@gmail.com and we'll build a package around your group.

Are there any discounts?

Active duty, veterans and first responders get 10% off with valid ID. Gift vouchers are available online any time.
